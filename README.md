# packingTracker
The Challenge: In daily warehouse operations, a lack of exact visibility into what merchandise gets loaded onto each transport unit leads to delivery discrepancies, time-consuming audits, and hard-to-trace customer claims.

The Solution: I developed an operations-focused platform to ensure end-to-end shipment traceability. The system identifies every box using a unique ID, linking it directly to its packing slip folio, the itemized list of products inside, and the assigned transport unit.

The Impact: With this tool, logistics teams can audit in seconds the exact departure timestamp of any load, which vehicle transported it, and the precise breakdown of its contents—eliminating blind spots in the supply chain and streamlining dispatch validation.
